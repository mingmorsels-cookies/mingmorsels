Shipping Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Delivery Areas

We currently offer delivery across select pincodes in Bengaluru, Mumbai, and New Delhi. For specific delivery availability, please enter your pincode during checkout.

2. Estimated Delivery Times

Because our products are baked fresh daily, delivery times vary by location:

  • Bengaluru (Local): Same-Day Delivery (Within 2-4 Hours)
  • Mumbai & New Delhi: Express Delivery (Next Day)

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated dynamically at checkout based on your delivery pincode and order volume. We occasionally offer free shipping promotions on bulk or premium orders.

4. Tracking Your Order

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email containing a tracking link. You can also use our Track Order page to check the real-time status of your delivery.
