🔄 Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 2026 • Miora Delights Private Limited

1. Order Cancellation Window

Because our cookies and muffins are baked fresh to order in dedicated daily baking batches (10 AM, 2 PM, and 6 PM), orders may be canceled within 1 hour of placing the order or before the order enters the baking dispatch process.

2. Refund Process & Timelines

For all approved cancellations or failed payment transactions, 100% of the transaction amount will be automatically refunded back to your original payment source (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, Net Banking) within 3 to 5 business days.

3. Perishable Food Return & Replacement Policy

Due to FSSAI food safety regulations, delivered food items cannot be returned. However, we offer a 100% Free Replacement or Full Refund if:

  • Your order package arrives damaged, tampered with, or leaking.
  • Incorrect items were delivered compared to your invoice.
  • Items fail our high quality standards upon arrival.

To claim a replacement, please email photos of the package to support@mingmorsels.com or message our WhatsApp Helpline within 24 hours of delivery.

Effective Date: February 2026 • FSSAI License No. 10022011000456

1. Corporate Ownership

This digital platform is owned and operated by Miora Delights Private Limited. By accessing this website or purchasing our products, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

2. Baking Slot & Product Fulfillment

All items are handcrafted in small artisanal batches. Baking slot selections (e.g., Tomorrow Morning 10 AM Batch) represent target baking cycles. Delivery times are estimated and may vary slightly due to local weather or courier traffic conditions.

3. Ingredients & Allergen Advisory

Our baked goods contain pure cow butter, organic raw apiary honey, whole grains, nuts (almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios), and natural extracts. While we offer eggless and gluten-aware varieties, all goods are baked in a facility that processes dairy and tree nuts.

4. Secure Online Payments

Online payments are processed securely via Razorpay Payment Gateway using 256-bit SSL encryption. Miora Delights Private Limited does not store or process your credit card numbers or banking passwords.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery Policy

Fresh Daily Batch Delivery • Miora Delights Private Limited

1. Fresh Baking & Continuous Fulfillment

Our cookies and muffins are baked fresh continuously every day upon order placement. Orders are packaged warm into air-tight protective barrier pouches immediately upon cooling, sealed, and dispatched via our automated Shipway logistics network.

2. Delivery Timelines

  • Same-Day Local Express: Delivered within 3–5 hours of baking slot completion.
  • Standard Metro Delivery: Delivered within 24–36 hours across major Indian cities.

3. Delivery Charges

Free Express Shipping is provided on all orders of ₹1,000 and above. A nominal delivery fee applies to smaller orders based on destination.
