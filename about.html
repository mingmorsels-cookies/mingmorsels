The Story Behind mingmorsels

Crafting luxury cookies and gourmet baked treats with 100% pure cow butter, distinctive flavour combinations, and zero margarine.

The mingmorsels Promise

Pure Butter. Distinctive Flavours. Memorable Moments.

At mingmorsels (Miora Delights Private Limited), baking is more than a culinary process — it is an art form designed for when moments matter. We believe every bite should turn an ordinary occasion into an unforgettable sensory experience: a delicate balance of rich buttery crunch, botanical aromatics, and clean wholesome goodness.

Founded in Bengaluru with a passion for unyielding quality, mingmorsels set out to redefine the cookie and muffin landscape. We noticed that too many market treats relied on hydrogenated fats (dalda), artificial preservatives, chemical extenders, and excessive refined sugars. We knew connoisseurs deserved a purer, more luxurious alternative.

Our Artisanal Process

Every single cookie and soft-baked muffin is handcrafted in small artisanal batches to ensure absolute perfection. From slow-roasting whole California almonds to extracting the delicate essence of organic Damask rose petals, we oversee every step with master artisan precision.

We bake continuously every morning upon order placement, ensuring the treats delivered to your doorstep are as fresh, fragrant, and crumbly as the moment they left our central studio ovens.
