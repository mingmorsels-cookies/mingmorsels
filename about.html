The Story Behind mingmorsels

Crafting luxury cookies and gourmet muffins in Bengaluru with pure cow butter, natural plant Stevia, and uncompromising artistry.

The mingmorsels Promise

At mingmorsels, baking is more than a culinary process — it is an art form. We believe every bite should be a sensorial experience: a delicate balance of rich buttery crunch, botanical aromatics, and clean sweetness that transports you to a moment of pure bliss.

Founded in Bengaluru with a passion for unyielding quality, mingmorsels set out to redefine the cookie and muffin landscape. We noticed that too many market treats relied on hydrogenated fats (dalda), artificial preservatives, chemical extenders, and excessive refined sugars. We knew connoisseurs deserved a purer, more luxurious alternative.

100% Pure Ingredients • Zero Compromises

Zero Margarine. Zero Dalda. Zero Palm Oil. Zero Refined Sugar. Every batch is slow-baked with 100% pasture-fed cow butter, natural plant-based Stevia, and genuine terroir botanicals.

Our Artisanal Process

Every single cookie and soft-baked muffin is handcrafted in small artisanal batches to ensure absolute perfection. From slow-roasting whole California almonds to extracting the delicate essence of organic Damask rose petals, we oversee every step with master artisan precision.

We bake continuously every morning upon order placement, ensuring the treats delivered to your doorstep are as fresh, fragrant, and crumbly as the moment they left our central studio ovens.

Crafted for Every Sweet Occasion

Whether you are treating yourself to a serene evening with our Stevia Sugar-Free California Walnut cookies, sharing our Belgian Dark Chocochip muffins with family, or sending our signature luxury gift tins to clients across India, mingmorsels elevates every moment.

Thank you for allowing us to be part of your sweetest celebrations.
