Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

1. Order Cancellations

Due to the perishable nature of our freshly baked goods, orders can only be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. Once preparation or baking has begun, we are unable to accept cancellations or provide a refund.

2. Refund Eligibility

We take immense pride in the quality of our cookies and muffins. Refunds or replacements are only issued under the following circumstances:

  • You received an incorrect item.
  • The product arrived severely damaged or compromised.

3. Reporting an Issue

If you experience any issues with your order, please contact us at mingmorsels@gmail.com within 24 hours of delivery. Please include your Order ID and photographic evidence of the issue. We will review the case and, if approved, process a refund to your original method of payment within 5-7 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Gift cards and promotional items are non-refundable.
